06 COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we begin.

Not sure what you need yet? Start with a conversation. We’ll discuss the scope and recommend a suitable approach.

How do we get started?

Send an enquiry with your business details, goals, service requirements and any relevant deadline. We’ll review the information and discuss the next steps.

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on scope, deliverables, production requirements, timelines and advertising spend. We can prepare a proposal after understanding your needs. Media spend and third-party costs should be clearly itemised.

Can I combine multiple services?

Yes. You can enquire about a combination such as social media management, video production, design and paid campaigns. The proposal should clearly define what is included.

How long does a project take?

Timelines vary by project complexity, feedback cycles and approvals. We’ll agree on milestones and delivery expectations before work begins.

Do you guarantee reach, leads or sales?

No ethical agency can guarantee a specific business result in every situation. We can define objectives, track agreed metrics and use the available data to improve campaign decisions.

Can you work with businesses outside Nellore?

Tell us where your business is based and what support you need. We’ll confirm project fit, logistics and whether the work can be handled remotely or requires an in-person shoot.

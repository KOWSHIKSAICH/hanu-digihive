Privacy Notice.

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This notice explains how HANU DIGIHIVE may handle information you submit through this website. Review and update this notice to match your actual business practices and applicable law before launch.

Information you provide

If you submit an enquiry, you may provide your name, business or brand name, email address, phone number, selected service, budget range, expected timeline and project description.

How information is used

  • To respond to enquiries and discuss potential projects.
  • To prepare proposals and communicate about requested services.

Form service and third parties

The website form is configured to use FormSubmit to deliver enquiries by email. When enabled, submitted information is processed by that service according to its own terms and privacy policy. You can also contact the agency directly by email or WhatsApp.

Retention and security

Information should be retained only for as long as needed for the enquiry, business relationship, legal obligations or legitimate recordkeeping. No website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

For questions about an enquiry or to request an update or deletion where applicable, contact info@hanudigihive.com.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when website functionality or data practices change. Confirm that the details here accurately describe your processes before publishing.
